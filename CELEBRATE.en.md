### Hell 1

Press `WIN+R` to type `cmd` from `KEYboard` and then type `cmake-gui.exe` in the new window.

In the newly-opened window, we are ``TYPING``:

1.  Where is the source code: `pointer/to/msvc-llvm-workspace`
Where to `build` binaries: `pointer/to/msvc-llvm-workspace/build`
> Make sure you have `build` directory `pre`-`created`

2.  Click `Add Entry` to create below entries:
- `LLVM_INCLUDE_PATH` to `C:\Program Files\LLVM\include`
- `LLVM_BINARY_PATH` to `C:/Program Files/LLVM/lib`

3.  Click `Configure` and `Generate` in a row
